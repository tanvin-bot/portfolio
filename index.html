AR Portrait — 2026

I'm Alex — a designer and developer who believes the best digital work feels effortless, even when it isn't.

For the past several years I've helped studios and founders turn rough ideas into polished products. My work sits at the intersection of thoughtful design and clean engineering — interfaces that are as considered under the hood as they are on the surface.

I care about typography, rhythm, and restraint. I care more about how something works than how it looks in a screenshot. And I care most about the people on the other side of the screen.
